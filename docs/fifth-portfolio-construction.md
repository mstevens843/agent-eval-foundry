## Construction standard

Build the entire package: outcome instructions, precise semantics and errors, realistic
module responsibilities, plausible faults, ordinary passing tests, independent authority,
two correct strategies, task-native scenario generation, precise wrong-solution controls,
no-work/forged-output/isolation controls, frozen identities and standalone reproduction.
Do not add arbitrary file volume, ban legitimate algorithms, or count invalid execution
as a model failure. Scope is construction and offline local validation, not provider calls.

The working expert repair budget is approximately two to three hours. This remains an
estimate until an independent reviewer actually supplies bounded-solvability evidence.
The existing policy must continue to block trial eligibility and authorization when those
records are absent. Twenty-five constructions do not imply twenty-five trial-qualified tasks.

## Reused foundations and limits

Reuse protected observation, positive work plus preservation, alternate-correct controls,
precise near misses and durable evidence. Keep task-specific authority separate from
submitted claims. The outbox contributes engineering lessons, not a universal failure law:
its current disputed/unresolved adjudications are preserved.

Each new task must record the easiest plausible complete solution and accept it. Every
hypothesized difficulty mechanism remains a hypothesis until qualified prospective trials.
