# Current evidence interpretation

This is the current interpretation of preserved records. Older phase reports and the memo describe decisions made then; their assertions are not current qualification certificates. Original trial bytes and adjudications are retained.

## Durable outbox

Six completed zero-reward outcomes are preserved. Current adjudications classify five as specification ambiguity and leave one unresolved. All six agents wrote checks. The earlier narrative that Codex wrote no checker, or that these outcomes conclusively establish six fair capability failures, is withdrawn. See [the versioned case](../findings/cases/durableoutbox-cc267/000001.json) and [read-only inspection](evidence-learning.md).

Independent effects, meaningful positive work, legal history, attainable progress and strong near-correct controls remain useful engineering lessons. Applying them to another domain is a construction hypothesis until that package earns its own evidence. They do not prove a universal personality difference between model families.

## Memory and replay

The repeated memory failure signature included a host-contract defect. Runs repaired by a compliant host are not replicated model weakness. The historical UI recorder family and the separate live-DOM trial have different contracts, model coverage and adjudications. Neither licenses a broad exact-model/effort claim for the new browser package.

## Construction and probability claims

Phase 19's registered research-corpus hash does not match the bytes preserved in Git. Both the initial committed corpus and current corpus have the newer hash; the registered original was not found. Retained ranking and captured candidate packets remain hash-checked historical evidence, but the old ranking cannot be freshly rederived from its original inputs. The strict rederivation path refuses the mismatch. Retrospective readers do not create missing packets or authorize current builds/trials. Similarly, Phase 17 package-control numbers describe its frozen package, not today's corrected CAA implementation.

Historical solve-probability tables are uncalibrated priors, not universal facts, forecast yields or a reason to reject every other professional task. Self-verifiability is not disqualifying: a fair task may have an exact checker, and agents may construct one. The useful question is whether realistic interactions expose incomplete implementations—not whether verification can be made impossible.

The present production funnel builds complete coherent packages before expensive decomposition. Legacy probe/scaffold workflows remain research/calibration tools. A thousand renamed interfaces, scenario variants or identical repairs would not be a thousand distinct deliverables.

Current qualification requires independent fairness/time evidence, trusted local assurance, exact observed profiles, substantive valid trials, and destination acceptance. Simulations, regrades, stale-package results, and imported labels without sufficient provenance cannot substitute.
